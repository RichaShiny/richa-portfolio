About me

I like problems where the data is imperfect and the decision still has to be made.

I’m a New York City–based Data Scientist focused on applied machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation, model evaluation, and data infrastructure. I currently work as a Research Data Scientist at CUNY’s Institute for State and Local Governance, building AI-powered legal charge classification systems and geospatial analytics pipelines.

I tend to build around the full decision loop: data → evidence → model → confidence → action → evaluation.
